Butterfly Valves

Leakage proof Operation

Butterfly valves are used for isolation and regulation of flow in industrial air, gas and process systems. Their compact design and reliable sealing help support dust collection systems, process ventilation and fluid handling applications.

Aircon Systems supports industrial customers with suitable valve selection for clean air handling, dust control, process systems and utility lines.

Applications

  • Air Handling
  • Dust Collection Systems
  • Process Utilities
  • Industrial Flow Control
