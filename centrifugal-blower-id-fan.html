Centrifugal Blower / ID Fan

Performant, High Pressure / Flow blowers

Centrifugal fans and blowers draw air into the inlet of the blower housing, through the wheel and discharge it through the discharge outlet. Their role is to move process air, fresh air and dust-laden air through dust collection and process systems.

Pressure blowers are designed for high pressures measured in static pressure water gauge. Volume blowers move larger volumes of air at lower static pressure. Aircon Systems supports industrial requirements through suitable impeller design, pressure performance and material selection.

Applications

  • Dust Collection Systems
  • Ventilation & Exhaust
  • Process Air Systems
  • Industrial Air Handling
