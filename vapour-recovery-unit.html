Vapour Recovery Unit

A Vapour Recovery Unit removes unwanted vapors from crude oil or distillate tanks and supports emission regulations. VRU systems can be designed for storage tank vapor capture and treatment in chemical and process industries.

A typical VRU includes a gas compressor, scrubber, variable frequency drives and switching devices used to manage gas flow and recover vapors for safer process conditions.

Applications

  • Storage Tanks
  • Crude Oil Facilities
  • Chemical Plants
  • Emission Control
