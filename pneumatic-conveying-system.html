Pneumatic Conveying System

Safe transporter for bulk materials

A pneumatic conveying system transfers dry bulk materials and powders through an enclosed pipeline using pressure differential and air flow. This provides a cost-effective way to move powders and granular materials with low material loss.

Aircon Systems supports systems for food and beverage, chemical, detergent, renewable and specialist material industries.

Applications

  • Food & Beverage
  • Chemicals & Detergents
  • Pet Food
  • Renewables
