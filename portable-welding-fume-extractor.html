Portable Welding Fume Extractor

High-Performing Welding Fume Extraction

A portable welding fume extractor pulls out harmful fumes that are generated during welding operations. It is compact and designed to reduce airborne welding smoke while using lower energy and maintaining ease of use.

These systems are useful in workshops and process areas where localized fume capture improves workplace safety and operator comfort.

Applications

  • Welding Shops
  • Fabrication Units
  • Maintenance Areas
  • Industrial Workstations
